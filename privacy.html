Privacy Policy — Ajan Farm

Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. Who we are

Ajan Farm is a personal, self-hosted suite of AI assistants ("the App") created and operated by Gökhan Pınar ("the Operator"), gohikan@gmail.com. The App runs solely for the benefit of the Operator and the dedicated Google accounts he authorizes.

2. What data the App accesses

With the explicit OAuth consent of the Google account holder, the App may access and act on data in the authorized Google Workspace services: Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Tasks, Google Drive, Google Contacts (People), Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Forms. The exact scopes granted are shown on the Google consent screen at authorization time.

3. Where data is stored

OAuth tokens and the data the App processes are stored exclusively on the Operator's own private infrastructure (self-hosted servers under the Operator's control). No data is sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No third-party analytics or tracking is used by the App.

4. Purpose of processing

The App processes Google Workspace data only to perform task automation requested by the account holder — e.g. summarizing mail, managing calendar events and tasks, organizing Drive files, and handling documents and forms — on that account holder's behalf.

5. Data retention and deletion

Data is retained only as long as needed for the automation tasks. The account holder may revoke OAuth access at any time from their Google Account permissions page; upon revocation, stored tokens are invalidated. To request deletion of any locally stored data, contact the Operator by email.

6. Contact

Questions, requests or complaints: gohikan@gmail.com
