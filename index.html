Ajan Farm

Personal project Automation

Ajan Farm is a private, self-hosted suite of personal AI assistants (agents) created and operated by Gökhan Pınar. Each agent automates everyday digital tasks — reading email, managing calendars and tasks, organizing files, and handling documents — on behalf of its single owner, using Google Workspace services (Gmail, Calendar, Tasks, Drive, Contacts, Docs, Sheets, Forms) through Google's official OAuth-based APIs.

How it works

  • Every agent connects to one dedicated Google account authorized by the owner via OAuth consent.
  • Access is personal: agents act only for the account owner who granted consent.
  • OAuth tokens are stored privately on the owner's own infrastructure and are never shared or sold.
